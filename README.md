This app is a teams generator and games played tracker, designed to help organize pickup games, sports groups, or casual tournaments fairly.

Here is a summary of its key features:
- Preset & Custom Rosters: Easily load predefined player lists for different days of the week (Monday, Thursday, Saturday, Sunday, etc.) or start with a custom/empty list and add players on the fly.
- Availability Tracking: Use checkboxes to mark which players are present and available to play for the current session. A "Select All" option makes bulk updates quick.
- Smart Team Generation: Automatically splits available players into two balanced teams based on:
  - Games Played: Prioritizes players with fewer total games to ensure fair playing time.
  - Rotation: Prioritizes players who sat out or didn't play in the previous game over those who just played.
  - Randomization: Randomly selects among players tied in stats to keep matchups fresh.
- Live Games Tracker: Keeps a running tally of how many games each player has participated in during the session, with manual + and - adjustment buttons for corrections.
- Games Played History: Logs all accepted game matchups (Team 1 vs. Team 2) sequentially at the bottom of the page.
- Mobile-Friendly Design: Optimized with responsive layouts, touch-friendly buttons, and flexible stacking containers so it works seamlessly on smartphones and tablets.
- Persistent Data Storage: Automatic save/restore of rosters, players, games played, player availability statuses, roster selection, and game histories across sessions.
