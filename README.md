# Teams Generator & Games Played Tracker

A simple app to help organize pickup games and make sure everyone gets a fair number of turns to play.

## What it does

**Pick your group**
Choose from your regular groups (Monday, Thursday, Saturday, Sunday, etc.) from a dropdown, or start with a blank list and add your own players. Whatever you set up is saved automatically — close the app and come back later, and everything's just how you left it.

**Add players easily**
Type a name and hit Add — new players slot into the list alphabetically so they're easy to find.

**Mark who's here**
As people show up, check them off. Checking someone as "available" automatically marks them as checked in too — one tap covers both.

**Fair play, built in**
The app remembers how many games everyone's played and, importantly, who's been sitting out the longest. When you generate teams, it prioritizes:
1. Players who've sat out the most games in a row
2. Among those, players with fewer games played overall

This means nobody gets stuck watching from the sidelines game after game, and latecomers who join partway through a session get folded in fairly rather than jumping the line or getting unfairly held back.

**Generate teams in one tap**
Set how many players you want per team, hit "Generate Teams Preview," and you'll get two balanced teams instantly. Don't love the mix? Just tap again for a new draw.

**Track every game**
Once you're happy with the teams, hit Accept and it's logged — with a timestamp — in a running history you can scroll back through anytime. Made a mistake? Any game can be deleted, and the app will correctly adjust everyone's play count.

**Start fresh anytime**
The Reset button wipes the current group's session back to a clean slate — handy for starting a brand new day of play.

## Why it's useful

It takes the mental math and arguments out of "who sits out next" — the app tracks that automatically so game organizers can focus on the game, not the spreadsheet.
